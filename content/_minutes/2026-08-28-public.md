## Other questions

### Do we want to adopt the current draft of internal policy?

[The November 2025 MGM agreed][meeting1]
(as part of a discussion about ban automation)
that it would be beneficial to have an _internal_ document which records
staff consensus on when elevated privileges may be used and what the
internal transparency requirements for them are. This document would help
consolidate generally accepted practices that were previously scattered
across emails, meeting minutes, internal documentation, and training.
A draft was made soon after, but never formally adopted.

The meeting agreed that substantial changes to this document should require
meetings to approve. A few last-minute changes were also proposed and
approved. Afterwards, the meeting voted unanimously to adopt this document.

[meeting1]: /minutes/2025-11-28-public#should-we-forbid-the-use-of-network-ban-automation-that-staff-cannot-disable

### Should we enable bot mode?

While [the previous meeting generally agreed][meeting2] that requiring
LLM-enabled bots to use bot mode was desirable, the decision to amend
policy to that effect was deferred. Therefore, there wasn't
adequate formal consensus to support bot mode on Libera.Chat.

Solanum's implementation of bot mode is split into three modules:
one that provides the mode itself, one that prevents the mode from being
changed while joined to channels, and one that adds a channel mode that
prevents clients with bot mode enabled from joining without an invite/invex
or speaking without voice/ops. The stated advantage of loading all three
is that it is self-evident that a bot is permitted in a channel if
both the client and the channel have their respective bot-related modes set.

The meeting reaffirmed that bot mode can be useful to have from a policy
standpoint, which wasn't the case during
[the July 2024 meeting which first discussed bot mode][meeting3].
The meeting ultimately voted unanimously to load all three modules.
However, as some recent attempted IRCv3 feature deployments faced
unexpected issues, the meeting agreed that the modules would be tested
further before being loaded on the whole network.
